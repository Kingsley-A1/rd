A voice rooted in R&B. A spirit moved by African rhythm.

Born Jaboro Treasure in 1987, Ray Dynasty is a UK-based Nigerian singer, songwriter and recording artist bringing together soulful melody, emotional honesty and contemporary African influences.

His musical journey began in 2006. Years spent as a vocalist, guitarist, church music director and live performer have shaped an artistic identity that feels both deeply personal and unmistakably versatile.

Drawing from R&B, Afrobeats, Gospel and acoustic music, Ray creates music grounded in expression, authenticity and connection.

Artist biographyFull length

Jaboro Treasure, born in 1987 and professionally known as Ray Dynasty, is a UK-based Nigerian singer, songwriter and recording artist whose sound blends the soulful depth of R&B with the vibrant rhythms of African music. With a passion for musical expression and a versatile artistic identity, he is carving out a distinctive space within the evolving Afro-R&B scene.

Drawing from his musical roots in R&B, Afrobeats, Gospel and acoustic music, Ray Dynasty brings a rich blend of melody, emotion and cultural influence to his artistry. His musical journey, which began in 2006, has been shaped by years of experience as a vocalist, guitarist, church music director and live performer. These experiences have helped him develop a sound that balances vocal expression, authenticity and contemporary African influences.

With his upcoming single “MISDEMEANOUR,” Ray Dynasty continues to strengthen his presence as an artiste with a passion for meaningful musical expression. The single represents another chapter in his artistic evolution, reflecting his desire to connect with listeners through music that captures emotion, personal experiences and relatable themes.

With “MISDEMEANOUR” marking an important milestone, Ray Dynasty is stepping forward with a sound, story and artistic vision of his own.
